Why timing matters

Early changes in memory and thinking can be subtle, and memory clinics have limited capacity for in-depth assessments.

Spotting who may be at higher risk can help care teams prioritise follow-up and discuss next steps with patients and families.

Current model

Assessing risk from routine visits

For patients with mild cognitive impairment (MCI), AlzhAI uses information care teams already collect to help clinicians decide who may benefit from further evaluation.

Request a demo ↗
What it uses
Medical history and results from neuropsychological assessments, such as tests of memory and thinking.
What it shows
An estimate of how likely a patient with MCI is to progress to dementia due to Alzheimer’s disease.
