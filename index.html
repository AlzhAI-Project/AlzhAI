Two AI Models. Complete Clinical Coverage.

Bridging the gap from routine screening to precision diagnostics.

Routine Clinical Data Model

Deep Learning applied to standard clinical inputs (health history and cognitive assesment) to detect Alzheimer's risk and stratify patients for further evaluation.

  • Accurate Screening
  • Interpretable Clinical Results

MRI CNN Analysis

Advanced Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for objective analysis of MRI scans, identifying invisible structural biomarkers like hippocampal atrophy.

  • Presymptomatic disease prediction
  • Automated structural volumetry
