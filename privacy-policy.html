Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This notice covers the AlzhAI public website and email enquiries. It is provided under Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible for your data

Daniele De Carli and Alberto Sudati, the co-founders of the AlzhAI project in Milan, Italy, are the joint controllers for this website and its contact mailbox. We share responsibility for the website and for handling enquiries and privacy requests. You can contact either of us or both at alzhai.project@gmail.com.

Visiting the website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. The pages load images, styles, scripts and fonts from the site’s own address and use no cookies, local storage, analytics or advertising trackers.

GoDaddy registers alzh-ai.com and provides its Domain Name System (DNS) service, which directs visitors to the host. When a DNS resolver looks up our domain, GoDaddy may receive technical query information such as the domain requested and the IP address of the system sending the query. See GoDaddy’s Global Privacy Notice for its data practices and retention criteria.

When your browser loads a page, it sends GitHub technical information needed to deliver it, including your IP address. GitHub states that it records and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement for its purposes and retention criteria.

We use hosting and DNS based on our legitimate interest in keeping the public website reachable and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Writing to AlzhAI

Contact is voluntary. The contact link opens your email app. When you choose to send an email, AlzhAI receives your email address, any name you provide and the message and attachments you send. Please keep your message to what is needed for your enquiry and leave out patient records and health information.

We use these details to answer and follow up on your enquiry, based on our legitimate interest in handling correspondence (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Article 6(1)(b) applies when processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you. Without a return address or enough information about the enquiry, we may be unable to reply.

We keep correspondence while the enquiry or related follow-up is open and delete it once it is closed. We keep relevant messages for longer only as needed to manage an agreement, meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Recipients and international transfers

GitHub and GoDaddy provide the services described above. Google provides Gmail, which handles the messages you send to AlzhAI. Both co-founders can access the inbox. Information may also go to competent authorities where disclosure is required by law.

GitHub, GoDaddy and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their notices describe the mechanisms they use, including the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and other safeguards where applicable. See the transfer information from GitHub, GoDaddy and Google. You can contact us to ask about the safeguards that apply and how to obtain a copy.

Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 21 GDPR, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, where applicable. Where processing relies on legitimate interest, you can object on grounds relating to your particular situation. We do not use this site or its mailbox to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Send requests to alzhai.project@gmail.com. We respond within one month. If we need to extend that period under Article 12(3) GDPR, we’ll tell you within that month and explain why.

You can lodge a complaint with Italy’s Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or the supervisory authority where you live, work or believe the infringement occurred.
